From church details to final handover.

Named-Church Licence

A licence to use Ministri — not a sale of the software.

The Fellowship Licence is granted to the authorised named church only for its own internal church and ministry activities.

The licensed code must not be:

  • sold, resold, rented or sublicensed;
  • shared, published or distributed to a third party;
  • deployed for another church, ministry, organisation or business;
  • transferred to another Potter's House church;
  • used to create a competing or derivative commercial software service;
  • presented in a way that removes or misrepresents Ministri ownership or branding.

Fellowship Exclusive

Available only to authorised Potter's House Fellowship churches.

Churches and organisations outside The Potter's House Fellowship are not eligible for the Ministri Fellowship source-code licence. The public Ministri product is the hosted Ministri Cloud service for web and mobile access.
